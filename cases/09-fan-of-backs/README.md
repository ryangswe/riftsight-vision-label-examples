# A fan of backs

In a fan of backs (an opponent's hand, for one), the back in front gets a back box if less than about half of it is hidden. The strips of the backs behind it share one ignore box, as long as that box does not cover most of the front back. See [How to label](https://github.com/ryangswe/riftsight-vision/blob/main/docs/labeling.md#card-backs).

![A drawing of a fan of backs](fan-of-backs.jpg)

*A drawing, not a frame: no frame we have shows a clean fan of backs labeled this way yet.*

Box 1 is the back in front: a back box around the whole card. The strips of the five backs behind it are one ignore box, 2, drawn up to the front back's edge.

When the strips show on two sides of the front back, one rectangle around them all would cover most of it. Draw two ignore boxes then, one per side.
